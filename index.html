Learn as if you will live forever, live like you will die tomorrow. - Mahatma Gandhi

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer & Information Science & Engineering at the University of Florida, where I work as a Graduate Research Assistant. I am a part of the TransSafeAI research group led by my advisor Dr. Hoang-Dung Tran. My research interests include formal verification of learning‑enabled cyber‑physical systems, and safe and secure artificial intelligence. I am a main developer of StarV, a qualitative and quantitative verification tool for learning‑enabled systems.

Before joining UF, I started my Ph.D. at the University of Nebraska-Lincoln (2021 – 2025) under the same advisor, and earned a B.S. degree in Computer Science from the University of Nebraska at Kearney.

Projects

NNV

Matlab toolbox for Neural Network Verification, using reachability methods for analyzing neural networks, particularly focusing on closed‑loop controllers in autonomous Cyber‑Physical Systems (CPS) (contributor).

StarV
