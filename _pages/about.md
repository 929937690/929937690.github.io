I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer & Information Science & Engineering (CISE) at the University of Florida, working as a Graduate Research Assistant in the [TransSafeAI research group](https://sites.google.com/view/v2a2/about) led by my advisor Dr. Hoang-Dung Tran.

My research interests include **formal verification of learning-enabled cyber-physical systems**, and **safe and secure artificial intelligence**. I am a main developer of [StarV](https://github.com/V2A2/StarV), a qualitative and quantitative verification tool for learning-enabled systems.

Before joining UF, I started my Ph.D. at the University of Nebraska-Lincoln (2021–2025) under the same advisor, and earned a B.S. in Computer Science from the University of Nebraska at Kearney.
